About this site

Sources, methods & caveats

A static, data-driven reference for Kerala Legislative Assembly elections (1982–2026), the 2024 Lok Sabha result in Kerala, and recent Assembly by-elections.

ⓘ Written analyses are AI-assisted. The prose commentary throughout this site is generated by an AI model from the underlying spreadsheets. Every quantitative statement — seat counts, vote shares, margins, swings and turnout — is computed directly from that data; the AI does not introduce numbers of its own. Even so, treat the commentary as informational, not authoritative, and verify against official Election Commission records before citing.

Data source

All results are derived from keralaassembly.org, an unofficial database based on official figures, compiled by journalist Roy Mathew. It is not an official site of the Kerala Niyama Sabha or the Election Commission.

The pages were collected page-by-page, parsed into spreadsheets, and converted to the compact JSON this site loads. Nothing here is re-scraped at view time — the site is fully static.

How the numbers are computed

  • Seats & winners come from each constituency's result page (winner, party, margin, runner-up).
  • Turnout is computed uniformly as valid votes ÷ electorate for every year, summed across all 140 constituencies, so the series is comparable across eras.
  • Vote share by front is computed by summing each party's votes across all constituencies and grouping parties into fronts.
  • Front (LDF / UDF / NDA) seat totals: from 2011 onward the source labels each constituency's front directly, and those labels are used as-is. For 1982 and 2001 the source publishes front-wise seat tallies (used directly), and for 1987 the source's "Brief" states them. For 1991, 1996 and 2006 the source does not publish front-level seat totals, so each winning party is assigned to its front for that election and the totals are summed. This classification method reproduces the source's own published front tallies for 1982, 1987, 1996 and 2001 exactly, which is why it is applied to the remaining years; even so, the 1991, 1996 and 2006 front splits are derived estimates and may differ by a seat or two from other tabulations.

Caveats

  • The source notes that official totals and the sum of candidate votes do not always tally (tabulation errors in the original figures); small discrepancies therefore exist.
  • Constituency names and boundaries changed with delimitation (notably in 2008, effective from 2011), so seat names are not comparable across that boundary.
  • Polling % and NOTA were not published for elections before 2011; where a field is unavailable it is shown as “–”.
  • Party-to-front allegiance shifts over time (several parties changed fronts between elections); front groupings reflect each election's alignment as best as the data allows.
  • 2026 figures are as published in the source database at the time of collection.
