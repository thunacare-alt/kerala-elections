About this project

A visual history of Kerala's elections

Every Kerala Legislative Assembly election from 1982 to 2026, the 2024 Lok Sabha result in the state, and recent by-elections — mapped seat by seat.

Scope

The Kerala Assembly has 140 seats. This project covers all ten general elections to it since 1982 — the result in every constituency, the share of the vote, turnout, and the government that followed — alongside the state's 20 Lok Sabha seats in 2024 and sixteen Assembly by-elections held between 2012 and 2025.

How the figures are computed

Every number on the site is computed from constituency-level results — nothing is estimated where a count exists.

  • Turnout is measured uniformly as valid votes divided by the electorate, summed across all 140 seats, so it is comparable across every election.
  • Vote share by front sums each party's votes across all seats and groups the parties into their fronts.
  • Seats by front use the front of each winning candidate. From 2011 onward the front is recorded per constituency; for 1982–2006 each winning party is grouped into its front for that election — a method that reproduces the known front tallies for 1982, 1987, 1996 and 2001 exactly, and so is carried back to 1991, 1996 and 2006.
  • Margins are the winner's lead over the runner-up.

Things to keep in mind

  • Constituency names and boundaries were redrawn in the 2008 delimitation (effective from 2011), so seats are not directly comparable across that line and some names change.
  • Polling percentage and NOTA were not recorded for elections before 2011; where a value is unavailable it shows as “–”.
  • Several parties have changed fronts between elections; front groupings reflect each election's alignment.
  • Figures are provided for reference and study, not as an official record.

Built as a static site with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript; charts rendered with Chart.js.
